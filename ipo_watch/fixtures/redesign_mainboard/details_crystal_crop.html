About Crystal Crop Protection IPO

Crystal Crop Protection IPO open date is November 18, 2026, and the IPO will close on November 20, 2026. Crystal Crop Protection IPO is a book-built issue. The company to raise around ₹455.71 crores via IPO that comprises fresh issue of 355 crores and offer for sale up to 37,02,694 equity shares with face value of ₹10 each.

Crystal Crop Protection IPO price band is ₹259 to ₹272  per share. The retail quota is 35%, QIB is 50%, and HNI is 15%. Crystal Crop Protection IPO is to list on BSE and NSE on November 26, 2026. The allotment of Crystal Crop Protection IPO date is November 23, 2026.

The company reported revenue of 3,180.98 crores in 2026 against 2,731.94 crores in 2025. The company reported profit of 144.91 crores in 2026 against profit of 118.39 crores in 2025.

About Company

Crystal Crop Protection is a crop solutions company that provides a wide range of agrochemicals and seeds. Its product range includes crop protection products such as herbicides, fungicides, and insecticides, as well as natural crop solutions like biostimulants, bioprotectants, plant growth regulators, liquid fertilizers, and micronutrients. It also provides seeds for field crops, vegetable crops, and flowers. Its products mainly cater to farmers in India, offering more than 174 different branded crop protection products in different pack sizes.

Crystal Crop also sells its products to several countries, including Bangladesh, Nepal, and parts of Africa and Asia. It also offers seeds for cotton, cereals, oilseeds, fodder crops, vegetables, and flowers, selling both in India and in international countries such as Japan, the USA, and Thailand. Furthermore, the firm operates an integrated business model with strong R&D, seed breeding, advanced technology, and a pan-India distribution network.
